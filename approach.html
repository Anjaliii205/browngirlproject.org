Our Approach

Research begins with listening.

The assumptions researchers bring into an investigation influence the knowledge they produce.

Rather than approaching women of color through predetermined narratives, Brown Girl Project begins with open-ended inquiry, allowing participants' experiences to shape the direction and interpretation of the research.

Our Research Process

Four interconnected stages.

Our approach combines qualitative and participatory research, narrative inquiry, and trauma-informed principles through four interconnected stages.

01

Inquiry

Developing research questions through preliminary research and exploratory conversations.

02

Investigation

Exploring lived experiences through interviews, focus groups, and facilitated conversations.

03

Interpretation

Identifying emerging themes, narratives, and differing perspectives through qualitative analysis.

04

Return & Reflection

Returning research to communities through accessible materials and opportunities for dialogue, reflection, and further inquiry.

Methodology

Listening Labs

Listening Labs are BGP's conversation-based participatory research methodology, designed to create spaces where women of color can explore their experiences, engage with different perspectives, and contribute to the interpretation of research.

Listening Labs can support different stages of an investigation, from developing research questions to reflecting on emerging findings.
